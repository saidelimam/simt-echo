## 2. Findings and proposed fix slices

### F1 — P1: actual phone hold angle produces a confidently wrong travel direction

**Owner:** W16-02. **Fix slice:** recover FOE independently of the common-depth/yaw nuisance fit.

Requirement: the held-off-axis phone's FOE/travel offset must be within 2°, and confidence must fall when the model cannot support a claim.

Locations: `shared/src/commonMain/kotlin/app/simt/echo/capture/visual/EgoMotionFit.kt:45`, `:49`, `:99`; `shared/src/commonTest/kotlin/app/simt/echo/capture/visual/EgoMotionFitTest.kt:205`.

The joint fit gives every block the same expansion coefficient and adds an unconstrained yaw term. Depth variation from an oblique wall is absorbed into that term and the horizontal intercept, corrupting FOE. Statistical yaw significance is not physical identifiability. Separating `yawSigmaRadPerSec` does not protect `hasFoe` or `travelOffsetRad` from this error.

Executed reproduction: corridor seed 1234567, camera `(0,57,1.5)`, yaw fixed at +10°, forward movement +0.08 m along world y over 0.125 s, no camera rotation. VisualFlow → EgoMotionFit reports **−24.614°**, versus true travel offset **−10°**, confidence **0.89964**, all **12 inliers**. With camera yaw −10°, it reports **+25.055°** versus +10°, confidence **0.89353**. Both errors exceed the 2° target by a wide margin.

The existing acceptance test leaves the camera wall-normal and moves diagonally instead (`EgoMotionFitTest.kt:209`). SimPhone's hold offset is used to choose the trajectory; it does not rotate the supplied SimPose. This preserves uniform depth and misses the failure. `docs/perf/ego-motion.md` acknowledges the depth/yaw ambiguity, but delegates only yaw validity to W16-03; the already-published travel angle is also wrong.

**Suggested fix:** estimate travel FOE using robust intersections of translation-flow directions, allowing different per-block depth scales, with the independently known camera rotation as a constraint. Keep radial expansion and residual-yaw inference separately qualified. Do not publish a trusted FOE solely because the shared-depth joint fit has low residual. Where direction is genuinely unobservable, expose absent/uncertain FOE and prevent heading consumers from treating radial confidence as direction confidence. A policy that simply suppresses all held-offset cases would require an explicit plan change, not a claim of meeting the 2° target.

**Acceptance:** extend EgoMotionFitTest with real SimPose camera rotations ±10° while travel stays forward, oblique/mixed-depth surfaces, and both signs of travel offset. Require ≤2° error on observable cases, explicit absent direction on ambiguous cases, unchanged forward-expansion and zero-allocation gates. Keep the existing uniform-depth tests too.

### F2 — P1: four coherent outliers can bias expansion above 15% while confidence exceeds 0.5

**Owner:** W16-02. **Fix slice:** make robust initialization and confidence resistant to outlier location.

Requirement: approximately 30% moving-billboard replacement must retain expansion within 15%; dynamic blocks must become outliers rather than bias the fit.

Locations: `shared/src/commonMain/kotlin/app/simt/echo/capture/visual/EgoMotionFit.kt:38`, `:49`, `:53`, `:84`; `shared/src/commonTest/kotlin/app/simt/echo/capture/visual/EgoMotionFitTest.kt:232`.

Executed reproduction: start with `egoMotionTestFlow(expansionPerSec = 0.16)` (12 exact static vectors, dt 0.125 s, texture 256, quality 1). Replace zero-based blocks **2,3,10,11** with `dx = 0.024`, `dy = -0.012`, the same coherent displacement used by the existing billboard fixture. The fit returns **0.19700116/s**, **23.13% error**, confidence **0.500524**, with eight reported inliers. Across all 495 four-block placements, **21** exceed 15% error while confidence is above 0.5. This is a vector-level robustness result, not a rendered crowd claim.

The first ordinary least-squares fit and first-pass yaw-significance decision are already contaminated. Three subsequent Huber passes do not reliably recover the static majority. The existing test exercises only blocks 1,2,5,6.

**Suggested fix:** use a deterministic, bounded, allocation-free robust initializer or trimmed/consensus hypotheses before the three IRLS passes; revisit the yaw model decision after robust weights stabilize. Derive confidence from consensus quality, conditioning and model stability, including spatial support, rather than the retained residual alone. A known biased solution must not cross the consumer's 0.5 threshold. Check the added work against the later combined cost budget.

**Acceptance:** parameterize the four-of-twelve placement sweep, several coherent velocities and contiguous billboard placements; retain ≤15% expansion error for the declared observable majority cases. Add regression assertions for 2,3,10,11 and for false high confidence. Preserve the existing rendered test and allocation probes. Do not fix this by changing the expected expansion or relaxing the 15% bar.

### F3 — P2: the accepted rotation range violates the 0.15 px residual bound

**Owner:** W16-01. **Fix slice:** bound or correct finite-rotation compensation.

Requirement: rotational flow cancels to ≤0.15 px RMS at the 120×90 level; accepted output must be translation-only.

Locations: `shared/src/commonMain/kotlin/app/simt/echo/capture/visual/VisualFlow.kt:58`, `:93`; `shared/src/commonTest/kotlin/app/simt/echo/capture/visual/VisualFlowTest.kt:269`.

The kernel accepts each rotation component up to **0.15 rad**, but subtracts a first-order small-angle displacement. The accuracy tests use only **0.01 rad**. The allocation test's larger prior is not an accuracy assertion.

Executed reproduction on the same seeded end-wall scene, with zero translation and exact rotation supplied: pure yaw **0.10 rad** retains nine blocks and **0.29704 px RMS** residual; pure pitch retains eight blocks and **0.27859 px**. At **0.15 rad**, the values are **0.62365 px** and **0.59061 px**. These are reported as translation rather than rejected. Measurements are base-level pixels, matching the plan's units.

**Suggested fix:** compute a finite-rotation pinhole prediction (and account for within-patch rotational warp if needed), or reject pairs whose approximation error can exceed the bound. Derive any conservative acceptance limit from focal length, block positions and combined rotation, rather than choosing a threshold from one fixture. Coarse search finds a displacement; it does not correct the model subtracted afterward.

**Acceptance:** add signed yaw/pitch and combined-rotation sweeps across the entire accepted domain, including FOV approximations. Every accepted observable pure-rotation pair must meet ≤0.15 px RMS; unsupported pairs must clear output and confidence. Retain ≤0.2 px block accuracy and zero-allocation probes. These experiments did not test roll, so roll correctness remains unproven here.

### F4 — P2: normal walking at the planned 2 Hz rate exhausts the translation search

**Owner:** W16-01, coordinated with W16-05/W18-03. **Fix slice:** support the declared frame spacing and walking-speed envelope.

Requirement context: the plan adopts 2 Hz Navigate analysis and 0.9–1.1 m/s simulated walking; the kernel must supply usable displacement in that ordinary regime.

Locations: `shared/src/commonMain/kotlin/app/simt/echo/capture/visual/VisualFlow.kt:97`, `:104`; `shared/src/commonTest/kotlin/app/simt/echo/capture/visual/VisualFlowTest.kt:266`.

Coarse search runs only when the **rotational** prior exceeds three pixels. With little rotation, translation is restricted to ±3 base pixels, regardless of elapsed time (pairs up to two seconds are accepted). Boundary minima are dropped. Existing forward fixtures move only 0.04 m over 0.125 s.

Executed reproduction: camera `(0,57,1.5)`, straight forward, no rotation, seed 1234567. A **0.5 m** advance over **0.5 s** (1 m/s, 2 Hz) leaves **two blocks** and fit confidence **0.04505**. A 0.125 m advance retains all twelve. The low confidence prevents a trusted wrong update in this reproduction, but ordinary walking loses visual assistance even in the acceptance test's textured scene.

**Suggested fix:** add a bounded translation-aware prior or coarse fallback when the fine search lacks valid interior matches. Alternatively, change acquisition/pairing to provide sufficiently close frame pairs while honoring the declared average duty budget. The current plan's literal “coarse only when rotational prior is large” needs a documented refinement if a translation fallback is chosen. Increasing the fine radius blindly can violate the cost budget.

**Acceptance:** cover 0.9–1.1 m/s at Record and Navigate spacings, with near/far surfaces and small rotation, requiring enough correct blocks for a usable fit in the declared observable domain. Keep oracle bounds, confidence honesty and zero allocation. Reconcile the minimum usable rate with the scheduler's future 1 Hz straight-walk mode before claiming that mode works.

### F5 — P2: simulated camera motion and its orientation witness describe different phones

**Owner:** W15-01. **Fix slice:** synthesize camera and inertial witnesses from one handset pose.

Requirement: the common simulator includes hand-held yaw/pitch wobble, hold offset and IMU/gyro drift, so visual de-rotation and heading fusion can be tested against independent truth.

Location: `shared/src/commonTest/kotlin/app/simt/echo/sim/SimWalk.kt:75`, `:89`, `:110`.

Rendered camera yaw includes hold offset and yaw wobble. MotionUpdate yaw includes route heading, drift, angle random walk, magnetic anomaly and noise, but **omits that handset wobble and offset**. A static hold offset affects direction; changing wobble affects the actual inter-frame rotation that de-rotation needs. The sample exports no separate gyro, gravity or pitch-rotation witness from the same handset trajectory. Thus its only supplied orientation stream cannot consistently compensate the rendered camera. The current deterministic tests check repeatability rather than this physical relationship.

Executed diagnostic: seed 104729, 5 m straight corridor; the maximum adjacent-sample difference between camera-yaw change and MotionUpdate-yaw change is **1.16079°**. That value includes deliberately synthesized sensor error; the more fundamental source-level defect is that deterministic handset wobble is omitted from the measured orientation before noise is added.

**Suggested fix:** retain travel heading as truth, derive ideal camera/body orientation and angular velocity from the same pose, then apply explicit sensor-specific drift/noise/anomalies. Add separate raw witnesses needed by pose classification and heading triangulation rather than disguising travel heading as handset orientation. Distinguish the current legacy sensor baseline from the corrected visual-capable simulator. Any baseline regeneration must be an explicit reviewed measurement change, not a silent JSON rewrite.

**Acceptance:** noise/bias-off fixtures prove camera and integrated gyro rotations agree at frame timestamps, and that changing handset wobble changes both. Hold offset must remain distinct from travel direction. Add seeded drift/anomaly tests with known inputs, and run SimWorldTest, MonteCarloGateTest and both baseline tasks after any approved baseline update.

### F6 — P2: the required broken-filter negative control never runs a broken filter

**Owner:** W15-01. **Fix slice:** validate the measurement pipeline with an actual faulty estimator.

Requirement: MonteCarloGateTest must prove a deliberately broken filter fails the gate, not merely prove the threshold comparator rejects a larger number.

Locations: `shared/src/commonTest/kotlin/app/simt/echo/sim/MonteCarloGateTest.kt:128`; `shared/src/commonTest/kotlin/app/simt/echo/sim/MonteCarloGate.kt:209`, `:233`.

**Suggested fix:** provide a small test-owned trial/estimator adapter, keeping the default real Navigator path. Run seeded walks through an intentionally biased/disconnected estimator, score its actual emitted state, and assert end-to-end gate failure. Preserve the comparator boundary tests. Name the raw sensor heading metric explicitly, and add a separately versioned fused-output metric when heading fusion becomes available; do not relabel the old numbers as fused accuracy.

**Acceptance:** a real output bias or disabled observation must fail arrival/coverage scoring, while the unchanged real engine reproduces its baseline. Add a negative control for the heading measurement seam before W16-03 uses it to claim ≤5° over ten minutes. The repair does not require prematurely implementing W16-03 or visual navigation.

## 5. Residual risks and boundaries

- **Plane-size seam, W16-05 integration gate:** Android currently decimates by **8** (`shared/src/androidMain/kotlin/app/simt/echo/platform/FrameTapAnalyzer.kt:170`), while SimWorld uses **4** and the default flow kernel expects 240×180. The new kernel also requires dimensions divisible by four, at least 160×120. No explicit analysis resolution is selected in CameraXRecorder. This mismatch predates visual wiring, so it is recorded as an integration risk rather than a missing W16-05 feature charged to these slices. Test real delivered sizes; preallocate an appropriate resampling/size adapter and transform intrinsics consistently, or mark unsupported channels unavailable. Do not introduce another camera owner or silently change recording costs.
- **Synthetic frame metadata:** SimWorld.render currently leaves the new FrameSample intrinsics/aligned-clock fields at their defaults. The future consumer test/replay adapter must supply proven simulated clock/calibration metadata; passing these samples straight to a clock-gated production consumer is not evidence that visual processing ran.
- **Heading gate scope:** current baselines use a 45 m checkpoint, raw sensor heading and straight arrival geometry; they are not ten-minute fused-heading evidence or a proof of visual gains across junctions and crowds. F5/F6 repair prerequisite test infrastructure; W16-03 and W17 still owe their own behavioral gates.
- **Device/visual scope unreviewed:** no handset recording/navigation, thermal/battery result, Camera2 calibration, runtime zoom/crop behavior, iOS capture, UI visual QA, field A/B, or full security/privacy audit was performed. Cached iOS compile outputs and host allocation probes do not close those claims. No Android assembly or full application suite was run; those are not required to commit this documentation-only review and W16-05 owns the new wiring assembly gate.
- **Scope left alone:** no production source, existing test, baseline JSON, plan tracker or W16-05 worktree was changed by the review. No push, deployment or migration was performed. Temporary probe source was removed before staging the review document.
